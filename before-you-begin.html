Before you begin

Clarity is a decision tool that helps you think through an important choice, one question at a time.

The questions draw on several mental models to help you consider what you want, what you’d give up, and what could happen next. You’ll also look beneath your first reasons to explore the hopes, fears, pressures and assumptions that may be influencing you.

How to work through it

  • Bring one choice you’re considering. Describe what you could choose to do and what you hope it would help you achieve.
  • Put your thinking into your own words. Use the guides and examples to look further, and include anything else that matters.
  • Be specific where you can. Where an “I don’t know yet” response is offered, use it to record uncertainty.
  • Work at your own pace. You can move between sections and revisit earlier answers as your thinking develops.

What you’ll leave with

Your answers come together in a decision overview, with your reasons, trade-offs and unanswered questions in one place. Clarity won’t tell you what to do. It helps you understand your choice well enough to make the decision yourself.
