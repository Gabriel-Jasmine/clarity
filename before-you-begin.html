How Clarity works

A guide to the tool, whenever you need it.

Clarity guides you through an important decision, one question at a time. You’ll write reflections and make selections about what matters, what you might give up, what could follow, and the hopes, fears or pressures influencing you.

The questions draw on several mental models to help you consider what you want, what you’d give up, and what could happen next. You’ll also look beneath your first reasons to explore the hopes, fears, pressures and assumptions that may be influencing you.

Clarity helps you explore whether to do this. You’ll consider alternatives along the way.

How to work through it

  • Start with the decision you’re trying to make. Focus on one action you’re considering and what you hope to achieve. For example, “I’m deciding whether to accept a new job in another city.”
  • Put your thinking into your own words. Use the guides and examples to look further, and include anything else that matters.
  • Be specific where you can. Where an “I don’t know yet” response is offered, use it to record uncertainty.
  • Work at your own pace. You can move between sections and revisit earlier answers as your thinking develops.

What you’ll leave with

Your answers come together in a decision overview, with your reasons, trade-offs and unanswered questions in one place. Clarity won’t tell you what to do. It helps you understand your decision well enough to make it yourself.
