# Shared fictional example

Adopted 19 October 2026. Build `20261019-shared-story` replaces the existing sample text only. The [full persona, story and exact replacement inventory](https://github.com/Gabriel-Jasmine/clarity-product-decision-notes/blob/main/SHARED_EXAMPLE_NARRATIVE.md) live in the product notes and are the canonical editorial reference.

The one use case is **I’m deciding whether to accept a higher-paying design job in another city.** The fictional person is a 32-year-old designer, unmarried and in a stable relationship, close to family and friends, who hopes to start an independent design studio within two to three years. A 30% salary increase could fund the business, but relocation, living costs, workload and time apart could affect that aim and their relationships. The person has not decided to accept. The offer deadline is two weeks; several important conditions remain unconfirmed.
