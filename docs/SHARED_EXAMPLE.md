# Shared fictional example

Adopted 19 October 2026. Build `20261019-shared-story` replaces the existing sample text only. The [full persona, story and exact replacement inventory](https://github.com/Gabriel-Jasmine/clarity-product-decision-notes/blob/main/SHARED_EXAMPLE_NARRATIVE.md) live in the product notes and are the canonical editorial reference.

The one use case is **I’m deciding whether to accept a higher-paying design job in another city.** The fictional person is a 32-year-old designer, unmarried and in a stable relationship, close to family and friends, who hopes to start an independent design studio within two to three years. A 30% salary increase could fund the business, but relocation, living costs, workload and time apart could affect that aim and their relationships. The person has not decided to accept. The offer deadline is two weeks; several important conditions remain unconfirmed.

## Things Dear to You — approved additions

After reviewing the focused mock, the owner explicitly approved this exception to the earlier replacement-only release. Add exactly two Example disclosures using the existing shared component, both closed initially.

### Life direction

Keep **What are you ultimately trying to build, protect, or become in your life?** unchanged. The intent is the personally envisioned fulfilling life, including how the person lives and who they become, rather than a list of career/business milestones. A business is one possible means to that life. The example is illustrative, not a scientifically universal ideal or required answer structure. Keep the existing unknown response and saved writing.

Guidance:

> Picture the life you would find most fulfilling. What would make it feel deeply worthwhile to you? Consider how you spend your days, the person you become, your relationships and what you contribute.

Example, after guidance and before the answer:

> I want to feel that the way I spend my days reflects what matters to me. I want to keep growing as a creative person, make things I believe in, and feel proud of what I contribute. I want a loving partnership and close relationships where we share ordinary moments and can count on one another. I want enough security and freedom to enjoy life, explore and be present with the people I love. Looking back, I want to feel that I used my abilities well, cared for my people, and lived a life I would choose again.

Use a native external label for the writing field; do not nest the interactive disclosure inside a label. Essential guidance stays associated with the field. The optional example is not its essential accessible description.

### Hard boundary

Keep **What must remain true, or what must not happen?** unchanged. Add one shared Example after the guidance/starters and before the repeated boundary fields, only when **Yes** is selected. Do not repeat it per boundary or add the financial boundary as a second sample.

> Before accepting, I need my partner and me to agree on a long-distance arrangement we both consider workable, including visits and when we would review it.

No other sections or samples change. Presentation rules remain Playbook 1.7.0. This supersedes only the earlier prohibition on these two specific slots; general replacement-only scope and all data/assessment contracts continue.
